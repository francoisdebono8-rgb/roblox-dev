# Awakening Design

Awakenings should change what a fighter can do, not just add damage and speed.

## General rule

An awakening should modify at least two of:
- movement rules,
- defensive rules,
- combo conversion rules,
- projectile behavior,
- resource economy,
- terrain interaction,
- target tracking,
- signature mechanic.

Avoid awakenings that are only `+X% damage`.

## Examples

### Itachi — Mangekyo Sharingan
Core shift: reading becomes control.
Possible changes:
- stronger prediction/counter windows,
- deceptive pressure becomes more oppressive,
- Tsukuyomi as setup/counter rather than raw spam,
- Amaterasu as area denial,
- projectile setups become harder to read.
Keep Susanoo out of ordinary neutral unless later proven necessary.

### Sasuke — Curse Mark
Core shift: disciplined aggression becomes feral aerial pressure.
Possible changes:
- more air routes,
- faster recovery into pursuit,
- stronger Chidori conversion,
- more aggressive Fire Style,
- altered movement posture,
- meaningful cost/limited duration.

### Naruto — One-Tail Cloak
Core shift: clone chaos becomes animal pressure.
Possible changes:
- less human locomotion,
- stronger recovery/endurance,
- chakra-arm extensions,
- unusual angles,
- faster re-entry after knockback.

### Minato — Yellow Flash Flow
Core shift: prepared marks become a combat network.
Possible changes:
- faster mark selection,
- chainable marked destinations,
- new FTG combo conversions,
- better recovery after teleport attacks,
- signature finisher access.
Do not turn him into an arbitrary stat monster.

### Neji — Perfect Field
Core shift: precise control becomes near-total local awareness.
Possible changes:
- improved counter geometry,
- more tenketsu effects,
- lower Rotation cost,
- better projectile reads,
- temporary weak-point targeting.

### Gaara — Partial Shukaku
Core shift: defensive control becomes oppressive territory control.
Possible changes:
- larger sand influence,
- heavier bind/throw options,
- stronger automatic defense with a clear resource constraint,
- altered locomotion and reach.

### Kakashi — Sharingan Uncovered
Core shift: adaptation accelerates.
Possible changes:
- repeated enemy options become easier to counter,
- contextual copy-response system,
- better reaction timing,
- more efficient elemental transitions.

### Orochimaru — White Snake Instinct
Core shift: spacing becomes even less trustworthy.
Possible changes:
- extended body ranges,
- unusual evasions,
- snake-assisted follow-ups,
- more underground/angle routes.

### Shisui — Phantom Flicker
Core shift: burst mobility becomes positional ambiguity.
Possible changes:
- more persistent afterimages,
- one fake position can become the real entry,
- stronger cross-ups,
- genjutsu-linked movement traps.

### Rock Lee — Gates
Core shift: physical limitations are temporarily removed at a cost.
Possible changes:
- much faster transition speed,
- larger chase windows,
- new physical routes,
- recoil/exhaustion afterward.

## Awakening design questions

Before implementation:
1. What rule changes?
2. What new decision does the player gain?
3. What new counterplay does the opponent need?
4. What visual/body-language change communicates the state?
5. What prevents permanent snowballing?
6. Does it preserve the character's core identity?

## Future framework requirement

Awakenings should be data-driven enough to configure:
- trigger conditions,
- duration,
- resource cost,
- stat modifiers,
- unlocked move branches,
- altered movement profile,
- altered projectile profile,
- altered defensive rules,
- exit penalties,
- VFX/audio hooks.

But character-specific rule changes should remain possible without forcing everything into generic numbers.
