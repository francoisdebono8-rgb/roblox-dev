# Commercial art brief

Use the **actual game silhouettes and forge** as references. No competitor logos, copied layouts or unlicensed assets. Deliver crisp high-resolution artwork in current Creator Hub required dimensions; check upload specifications when exporting. No small text, fake rewards or misleading weapon/combat claims. Review at phone thumbnail size before uploading.

## Icon

One legendary golden Runic Greatblade, oversized and tilted diagonally above the Village Forge anvil. Dark charcoal furnace behind; warm orange light from below; sparse sparks. The guard, broad blade and rune marks must survive small-size reduction. One dominant silhouette, strong edge separation, no words. Crop tightly; avoid a cluttered inventory collage. The weapon should resemble WeaponModel's Runic silhouette, not a photorealistic sword unavailable in game.

## Thumbnail 1 — Forge → Merge → Flex

Wide in-game workshop composition. Foreground anvil at lower left; glowing weapon revealed above it. Behind/right: three small merge ingredients visually converge toward a stronger result and a rack with displayed weapons. One orange focal light. If typography is added by a designer, only “FORGE → MERGE → FLEX”, large and separate from important silhouettes. No battle scene.

## Thumbnail 2 — Rarity progression

Three actual silhouettes in increasing scale: Common Iron Saber, Epic Obsidian Greatsword, Mythic Runic Greatblade. Warm metal → restrained violet → rose/gold contrast. Dark continuous forge backdrop. Large optional labels “COMMON”, “EPIC”, “MYTHIC”; no tiny stats, fake odds or unattainable glow.

## Optional thumbnail 3 — The armory

Player avatar beside a six-slot armory of recognizable exposed weapons. Show the gold Founder trim only if the art makes clear it is an optional cosmetic. Frame public collection/flex, never combat. Use a real rack screenshot as geometry reference. Keep the rack visually attainable.

## Deliverables / review

Layered source + final PNGs; textless alternative; approved asset provenance. Actual screenshot capture is preferable to invented game screens. Check icon at 64 px, thumbnail at mobile card size, light/dark page backgrounds and safe cropping. Do not upload automatically before visual approval.
