## Full description
Two platforms need a usable connection, not just a row of steps.

AccessForge turns Start and End markers into industrial access assemblies: straight stairs, two-flight switchbacks or level catwalks. Set dimensions, rails, landings and optional supports. Inspect the proposed geometry beside nearby platforms before Build.

When endpoints or settings change, compare Existing and Proposed, then Regenerate the managed assembly with Studio Undo. The result is editable Roblox Parts with saved assembly data.

Free stair and array tools are useful for simple repeated treads. AccessForge focuses on the complete endpoint-to-assembly workflow, including rails and repeatable edits.

Supported layouts only: no spiral stairs, terrain snapping, automatic collision or route validation. Nearby preview is bounded, not a full scene copy. Regeneration replaces managed geometry, including manual edits to it. No network requests, analytics or external account; assembly data stays in the place.

## Privacy and exact limits
- No HTTP, telemetry or external service/account. Generated parts and assembly metadata are stored in the current place and follow its sharing permissions. Preview is read-only; Build/Regenerate change the place.
- Straight, two-flight Switchback, straight level Catwalk only. No ladders, spiral/multi-flight designer, floor-boundary detection, terrain raycasts or collision/pathfinding validation.
- BasePart endpoint positions define the connection; endpoint size/rotation is not an automatic platform-fitting contract. Discrete step counts determine actual rise/tread.
- Maximum 1,000 parts and 256 steps; supported setting ranges are product limits. Switchback fit depends on width/endpoint separation; unsuitable layouts are refused. Catwalk endpoints must be level.
- Supports use a configured floor height, not terrain detection. Rails do not automatically join unrelated structures. Output is Parts, not a welded mechanism or gameplay script.
- Regeneration replaces managed geometry; manual changes inside it are lost. Unrelated additions are preserved subject to ownership/safety checks; unsupported descendants may block regeneration. Nearby supported-primitive context is bounded. No unlimited-world or simultaneous Team Create safety guarantee.
