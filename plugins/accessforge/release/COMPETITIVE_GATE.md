# AccessForge competitive gate

2026-09-27, issue #14. **GO for the focused integrated V1**, not a claim of
market exclusivity or proven sales. RoadForge remains on HOLD.

- [Quick Stairs](https://create.roblox.com/store/asset/6917372104): free acquisition,
  89%, 400+ votes, updated January 4, 2026. Its description copies a selected
  step with a drag handle. That alone would not justify another paid plugin.
- [Click to Build](https://click-to-build.trimatech.dev/docs/shape-builder): a
  broad construction suite. The [author's current post](https://www.reddit.com/r/robloxgamedev/comments/1wnbdyi/i_made_plugin_to_make_building_stuff_easier/)
  describes calculated/equalized straight stairs and spiral stairs. Interest
  in the post is not sales evidence.
- [Stairs Creator](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/stairs-creator-%E2%80%94-customizable-stairs-plugin/3815564)
  already advertises endpoints, automatic steps, rails, preview and Undo/Redo.
  Do not market those individual features as unique. Old replies reporting
  problems were not reproduced and are not used as current defects.

Focused searches for Roblox stair/railing generators, catwalk builders and
switchback plugins found no documented, actively maintained free equivalent
of the complete editable switchback + landing + catwalk + rails + supports
workflow. This is bounded public evidence, not proof that none exists.
Competitor code, geometry and UI were not inspected/copied or installed.

The paid position is assembly-level calculation and regeneration across these
related structures, not step duplication, generic shape creation, native Undo,
or an industrial recolor. Keep straight stairs, U-shaped stairs, landings,
catwalks, rails and baseline supports. Omit ladders, terrain fitting and new
structure modes. Ship only after the exact final artifact passes native tests
and the integrated workflow is visible in real Studio media.
