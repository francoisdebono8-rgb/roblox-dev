## Public title
Forge Playground - Try Five Studio Workflows

## Public description
Try five hands-on building and inspection demonstrations in a shared workshop.

Connect platforms with AccessForge. Reshape cables and repeated props with CurveForge. Inspect and repair sample connections with JointForge. Audit crate configuration with SchemaForge. Preview exact light changes with PresetForge.

Every station uses real product planning or validation code on sample objects. Review the result before Build, Apply or Repair; Reset starts that station again.

Demo only. The Forge Studio plugins work on your own projects in Roblox Studio. Playing does not install, unlock or grant ownership of a plugin. Workshop changes are temporary and are not saved after leaving.

Do not advertise device/multiplayer compatibility until the [public acceptance gates](PUBLIC_READINESS.md) pass. Do not add a public URL or available-now claim before publication.
